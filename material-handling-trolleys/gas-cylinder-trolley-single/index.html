Construction & Features

  • Fabricated from heavy M.S. round pipe, neatly bent, with an M.S. plate at the bottom and curved M.S. flats with “U” grooves.
  • A lockable safety chain is provided as a standard feature.
  • Suitable for CO2, O2, nitrogen and argon cylinders of 240 mm diameter × 1400 mm length.
  • Widely used for moving gas cylinders within factories, godowns, stores and warehouses.

The swivel castor on all models is a UHMW medium-duty swivel castor, 125 × 50 mm, with plain bore.
