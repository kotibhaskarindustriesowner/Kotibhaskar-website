Construction & Features

  • The platform is fabricated from a single piece of M.S. sheet, bent in six folds to form a “C” channel at the ends, with “U” grooves on the top surface for a neat appearance.
  • The platform is ribbed underneath with additional “C” channels for extra strength.
  • The push–pull handle is made from heavy M.S. round pipe and bolted on top of the platform.
  • Fitted with 2 fixed castors and 2 wheels mounted on a heavy-duty turntable assembly.
  • The turntable assembly combines a thrust bearing with a taper roller bearing for easy steering under heavy loads.
  • The compact turntable assembly with spring-loaded handle improves the manoeuvrability of the trolley.
  • Widely used for moving corrugated boxes, gunny bags, heavy materials, crates, etc. within factories, godowns, stores and warehouses.
