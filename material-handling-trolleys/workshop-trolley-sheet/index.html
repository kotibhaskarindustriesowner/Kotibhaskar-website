Workshop Trolley – M.S. Sheet Sides

Workshop trolley with all 4 sides closed by 1.2 mm M.S. sheet up to 450 mm height, for moving loose components and materials safely.

Construction & Features

  • The platform is fabricated from a single piece of M.S. sheet, bent in six folds to form a “C” channel at the ends, with “U” grooves on the top surface for a neat appearance.
  • The platform is ribbed underneath with additional “C” channels for extra strength.
  • All 4 sides are closed with 1.2 mm M.S. sheet with “U” grooves for strength, about 450 mm high above the platform.
  • The push–pull handle is made from heavy M.S. round pipe and welded on top of the platform.
  • Widely used for moving loose components, corrugated boxes, gunny bags, heavy materials, crates, etc. within factories, godowns, stores and warehouses.

Wheel Options

UHMW Ultra-High-Molecular-Weight PolyethylenePU-PP Polyurethane on PolypropyleneCI Cast Iron
Brakes Available with brakes and without brakes (optional swivel castor with parking brake).
