Construction & Features

  • Conveyor is with good quality endless rubber belt with hot vulcanized joint, chevron material, C - 15, thickness approx. 8 mm plus V at top approx. 15 mm.
  • Conveyor is movable on wheels with 4 nos swivel castors with parking brakes on 2 castors for stability.
  • Conveyor top is with guided side track at both sides to prevent falling of material.
  • A versatile motor powered conveyor is ideal for small product handling, machine feeding, unloading, transfer operations in processes, inspection or packing stations.
  • All rotating parts like motor, gear box drive, rollers, drums are guarded for safety.
  • Start, Stop, Forward, Reverse and Emergency Stop buttons are provided near gear box at one end.
  • Inclined Conveyor - Inclined Conveyor is provided with hydraulic pump assembly with cylinder for inclination of conveyor with hinged frame with locking pins over base for adjusting any desired height as per operators comfort. In normal leveled position, conveyor height is approx. 750 mm at both ends and during inclined position with hydraulic pump, maximum height of conveyor at other end can go upto 1800 mm.
