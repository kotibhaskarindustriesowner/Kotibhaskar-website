Construction & Features

  • Cage 800 x 800 mm with 750 mm side railing & 1200 mm back net for safety.
  • Self locked hinged door with torsion spring ensures operator safety during working.
  • Load capacity - Uniformly distributed load over cage on max. 0.8 x 0.8 mtr area.
  • Extra side stabilisers are provided for additional stability and safety.
  • Up & down control push buttons are in cage for adjustment of cage to any desire heights, which reduces operators strain, fatigue & increases safety, productivity in continuous picking application such as in stores, warehouses etc.
  • Wheels
    • a) Drive Wheel – Dia 230 x W 75 mm, Polyurethane – 1 No.
    • b) Front Wheel – Dia 150 x W 50 mm, Polyurethane – 2 Nos.
    • c) Support Castors – Dia 150 x W 50 mm, Polyurethane – 2 Nos. (Spring Loaded Castors)
  • Speeds
    • a) Travel Speed – Km / Hr – Laden 4 - 5, Unladen 5 - 6.
    • b) Lifting Speed – Mtr / Min – Laden 3 - 4, Unladen 4 - 5.
    • c) Lowering Speed – Mtr / Min – Variable Speed.
  • Battery
    • 24 V, 280 Ah, Lead Acid Tubular Traction Battery in fully charged condition (Heat Sealed) Capacity at C5.
  • Battery Charger
    • Self Compensating Microprocessor Controlled Battery Charger
    • Input - Single Phase 240 V AC Output - 24 V DC 24 V - 60 A - With 8 Hr Charging
  • Drive Unit
    • ZF make AC Drive unit assembly with traction motor 24 V – 1.3 kW, vertically mounted with reduction gear box with electromagnetic brake system.
  • Speed Controller
    • Curtis AC induction motor speed controller 24 V – 250 A with wire harness with 35 way AMPSEAL Connector and thermal switch with Albright contactors with safety fuses.
  • Hydraulic Power Pack
    • 24 V DC Power Pack with 2.2 kW DC Motor, 2.6 CC pump, relief valve, limit switch operated lever valve, with flow control valve.
  • Safety Devices
    • Belly safety switch, electric horn, battery low voltage indicator, maximum height limit switch and key switch are incorporated as standard features.
  • Stand on Platform
    • Provided with folding stand on platform so that operator stands on platform and drives the equipment. This reduces fatigue and increase safety of operator.
