Semi-Electric AC Hydraulic Stacker with Jib Hook

Construction & Features

  • Stacker - Operation
    • Up Movement - Push Button operated 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply Power Pack.
    • Down Movement - Precisely controlled Needle Valve
    • Pushing & Pulling - Manual
  • Electric Power Pack, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • AC Power Pack is with 2 HP, 3 Phase electric motor coupled with hydraulic pump, control valve, non return valve, needle valve, & oil tank with oil level indicator.
    • Electrical circuit is provided with MPCB, power on indicator, emergency off, contactor, push button, height limit switch & 3 pin plug with 3 mtr wire.
  • Stacker Pushing & Pulling Movement
    • Stacker pushing & pulling movement is manual with sprocket & chain operated handle with turn table assembly. This reduces pushing & pulling efforts by approx. 30 %.
  • Load Capacity - Uniformly distributed load on Jib Hook with maximum 1 x 1 mtr size
    • Load capacity of stacker gradually reduces by 30 % as height increases i.e. maximum safe load capacity of 1000 kg stacker is approx. 700 kg at maximum height, maximum safe load capacity of 1500 kg stacker is approx. 1050 kg at maximum height.
  • Wheels - UHMW Wheels with sealed ball bearings, White, UHMW
    • For 1000 kg - Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 82 x 70 mm - 04 Nos.
    • For 1500 kg - Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 82 x 70 mm - 04 Nos.
    • Foot operated parking brake to 1 steering wheel is standard feature.
  • Flame Proof Semi Electric Stacker, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • All above stackers are available in Flame Proof Semi Electric AC stacker 440 V.

Available in multiple configurations. Please refer to the specifications table below for details.
