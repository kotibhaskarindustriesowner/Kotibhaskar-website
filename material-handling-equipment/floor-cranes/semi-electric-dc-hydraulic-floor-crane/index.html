Construction & Features

  • Semi Electric DC Hydraulic Floor Crane - Operation
    • Up Movement - Push Button operated 24 V, DC, Battery Supply Power Pack.
    • Down Movement - Precisely controlled Needle Valve
    • Pushing & Pulling - Manual
  • Electric Power Pack, 24 V, DC, Battery Supply
    • DC Power Pack is with 3 HP, 24 V DC electric motor coupled with hydraulic pump, control valve, non return valve, needle valve, & oil tank with oil level indicator.
    • Electrical circuit is provided with Key switch, power on indicator, emergency off, 24 V DC heavy duty contactor, push button, height limit switch, battery level indicator & heavy duty charging socket for charger.
  • Battery - 24 Volts, 120 Amp Hr, Tubular Monoblock Battery (Battery 12 volts x 2 nos connected in series)
  • Battery Charger - 24 V DC
    • A separate 24 V, DC Battery Charger is needed to recharge the batteries.
  • Floor Crane Pushing & Pulling Movement
    • Floor Crane pushing & pulling movement is manual with sprocket & chain operated handle with turn table assembly. This reduces pushing & pulling efforts by approx. 30 %.
  • Load Capacity - Uniformly distributed load over 1 x 1 x 1 mtr pallet.
    • Load capacity of floor crane gradually reduces by 30 % as height increases i.e. maximum safe load capacity of 1000 kg floor crane is approx. 700 kg at maximum height, maximum safe load capacity of 1500 kg floor crane is approx. 1050 kg at maximum height.
  • Wheels - UHMW Wheels with sealed ball bearings, White, UHMW
    • For 1000 kg - Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 150 x 50 mm - 02 Nos.
    • For 1500 kg - Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos.
    • Foot operated parking brake to 1 steering wheel is standard feature.
  • Floor crane with manual adjusted telescopic boom from 1000 to 1500 mm at 100 mm pitch with locking pin.

Available in multiple configurations. Please refer to the specifications table below for details.
