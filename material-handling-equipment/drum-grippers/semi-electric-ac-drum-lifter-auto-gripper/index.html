Construction & Features

  • Standard Metal Drum - Dia 600 x Height 900 mm.
    • Drum Lifter is designed for standard metal drum dia 600 x height 900 mm.
    • In case of plastic drum or non standard drums you will have to send sample drum to Satara & it will be returned back along with equipment.
  • Single man operated equipment for vertical lifting & horizontal transportation.
  • Auto Gripper clamps the drum automatically without human touch to drum as well as unclamps the drum automatically when released without human touch to drum.
  • The chances of accidents due to improper handling of drums are totally avoided.
  • Safe handling of hazardous chemicals as human touch to drum is totally avoided.
  • The damages & wastages of contents are totally avoided.
  • Scientifically designed with built-in high quality & safety.
  • Applications
    • Used for loading & unloading of drums from truck in industries.
    • Used for keeping one drum above other drum, storage of drums in racks.
    • Largely used in chemical industries to handle hazardous chemicals.
    • Widely used for weighing of drums on weighing scale, keeping drums on pallets.
  • Wheels - UHMW Wheels with sealed ball bearings, White, UHMW
    • Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 150 x 50 mm - 02 Nos.
    • Foot operated parking brake to 1 steering wheel is standard feature.
  • Drum Lifter with Auto Gripper - Operation
    • Up Movement - Push Button operated 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply Power Pack.
    • Down Movement - Precisely controlled Needle Valve
    • Pushing & Pulling - Manual
  • Electric Power Pack, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • AC Power Pack is with 2 HP, 3 Phase electric motor coupled with hydraulic pump, control valve, non return valve, needle valve, & oil tank with oil level indicator.
    • Electrical circuit is provided with MPCB, power on indicator, emergency off, contactor, push button, height limit switch & 3 pin plug with 3 mtr wire.
  • Drum Lifter Pushing & Pulling Movement
    • Drum Lifter pushing & pulling movement is manual with sprocket & chain operated handle with turn table assembly. This reduces pushing & pulling efforts by approx. 30 %.
  • Flame Proof Semi Electric Drum Lifter, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • All above Drum Lifter are available in Flame Proof Semi Electric AC Drum Lifter 440 V
