Construction & Features

  • Pan size - 600 x 600 x 600 mm with taper outlet
    • Material Tilter is designed for Pan 600 x 600 x 600 mm.
  • Single man operated equipment for vertical lifting, horizontal transportation & 360 degree rotation of Pan.
    • Pan tilting is with worm & worm gear box which provides positive self locking at every position of tilt. Long hand lever for tilting provides effortless rotation of fully loaded pan without operator’s fatigue. The chances of accidents due to improper handling of material, damages, wastages of contents are totally avoided.
  • Material Lifter & Tilter - Applications
    • Material tilter is widely used for pouring materials in machine hopper, in reaction vessel, in plastic injection machine hopper, in sand moulding machine hopper etc.
  • Wheels - UHMW Wheels with sealed ball bearings, White, UHMW
    • Dia 200 x 50 mm - 02 Nos & Dia 150 x 50 mm - 02 Nos.
    • Foot operated parking brake to 1 steering wheel is standard feature.
  • Material Lifter & Tilter - Operation
    • Up Movement - Push Button operated 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply Power Pack.
    • Down Movement - Precisely controlled Needle Valve
    • Pan Rotation - Push Button operated 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply operated worm gear box
    • Pushing & Pulling - Manual
  • Electric Power Pack, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • AC Power Pack is with 2 HP, 3 Phase electric motor coupled with hydraulic pump, control valve, non return valve, needle valve, & oil tank with oil level indicator.
    • Electrical circuit is provided with MPCB, power on indicator, emergency off, contactor, push button, height limit switch & 3 pin plug with 3 mtr wire.
  • Material Tilter Pushing & Pulling Movement
    • Material Tilter pushing & pulling movement is manual with sprocket & chain operated handle with turn table assembly. This reduces pushing & pulling efforts by approx. 30 %.
  • Flame Proof Semi Electric Material Tilter, 440 V, 3 Phase AC Supply
    • All above Material Tilters are available in Flame Proof Semi Electric AC Material Tilters 440 V

Available in multiple configurations. Please refer to the specifications table below for details.
