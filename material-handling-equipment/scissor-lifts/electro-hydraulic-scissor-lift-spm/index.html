Construction & Features

  • Safety Features
    • Adjustable overload pressure relief valve acts as safety against overloading.
    • In case of power failure the table will remain in the same position & will not come down unless & until a non return valve is operated manually.
    • 100 mm safety clearance between top frame & bottom frame is provided to prevent trapping of human feet during scissor closing time.
    • All hoses & hydraulic joints are secured with double position clamps to add safety.
  • Structural Specifications
    • Flat, Angle, Channel, I Section – Mahaveer Steel / Equivalent
    • Square, Rectangular Tubes & Round Pipes – Apollo Tubes / Equivalent
    • Plain & Chequered Sheets, Plates – JSW Steel / Equivalent
    • All arms of scissor are fabricated from heavy thick rectangular tubes with extra ribbing flats near centre position to prevent deflection under load.
    • All pivoting joints are provided with Brass bushes to avoid wear and tear of pins and bush material & to increase the life of scissor lift.
  • Electrical Specifications
    • Electric Motor – 3 phase 440 V 4 Pole 1500 RPM – LHP / Equivalent
    • Switchgear, MPCB, MCB, Contactor – Ideal / Equivalent
    • Limit Switch, 24 V DC Push buttons, LED Indicators – Ideal / Equivalent
    • Power cables & wires – Polycab / Equivalent
    • Safety limit switch provided cut off motor supply at maximum lift.
    • SMPS provided in electric circuit gives 24 V DC current to all operating controls.
  • Hydraulic Cylinder Assembly & Rubber Buffers
    • Hydraulic cylinders are with honed tubes with end fittings with hard chrome plated rod, piston with polyurethane seals & PTFE guide rings.
    • Specially designed heavy duty Rubber buffer bushes are mounted on base to absorb shocks under heavy loads during scissor closing time.
  • Design Features
    • Guiding Members - Scissor Lift Table is fully guided in horizontal heavy channel with four rollers with phosphor bronze bushing inside to avoid wear & tear. This flat surface of angle without positive guiding results in high wear of rollers causing early maintenance, vibrations & play in table.
    • Overload Safety Feature - Overload safety valve provided in the circuit bypasses oil if scissor lift is overloaded by mistake & prevents damage to the main structure.
