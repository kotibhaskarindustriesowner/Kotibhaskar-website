PU-CI Wheels

PU-CI wheels have polyurethane bonded over a cast iron core by a casting process. They combine high load capacity and impact strength with low rolling friction, are floor-friendly and resist oil, grease and chemicals. Available as wheel only, fixed caster, swivel caster and swivel caster with brake.

Why PU-CI?

  • Polyurethane bonded/moulded over Cast Iron core — casting process
  • High load capacity / High impact strength / Low rolling friction / Floor-friendly
  • Excellent wear resistance
  • Resistant to oil, grease and chemicals
  • Widely used in Pallet Trucks, Stackers, Drum Tilters, Floor Cranes, Battery operated equipment
  • Cast polyurethane is MUCH BETTER than injection moulded — higher load capacity and service life
